## 3. The moment of misery

_The specific point where the product fails the user, forcing a manual workaround (Google, spreadsheets, group texts, a competitor)._

> StreamLine's massive catalog is deterring users from the app as it makes it difficult to find quality content and they no longer trust the platform to help them discover something worth watching.  Instead users are likely relying on other sources such as search engines, known platforms and publishings for movie reviews IMdB Rotten tomatoes, and exploring competitors for more personalized watchlists. The critical moment of misery occurs when a user opens StreamLine, spends too much time browsing, becomes frustrated, and leaves the app without watching anything.
